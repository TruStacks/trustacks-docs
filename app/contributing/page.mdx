# Contributing

This section is for people **working on TruStacks itself**: building and
running the whole platform from source. It is kept out of the main navigation
and out of search.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Not the customer path.** If you are using TruStacks, follow the
  [Quickstart](/getting-started/hosted-quickstart). You use the hosted service
  and install only the runner; there is no platform to operate.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **This is a public site, so "hidden" means soft-hidden.** These pages are
  out of the nav and asked to stay out of search, but anyone with the link can
  read them. Do not put anything here that must be private. Internal material
  belongs in the `trustacks-mvp` repo.
</Callout>

## In this section

- [Local development](/contributing/local-dev) · clone `trustacks-mvp`,
  `make up`, `make seed`, and iterate against the full platform on k3d. Also
  covers how local differs from hosted: push mode, Gitea deploy keys, Zot,
  and project-local policy signing.
- [Self-hosted quickstart](/contributing/self-hosted-quickstart) · not
  currently available.
