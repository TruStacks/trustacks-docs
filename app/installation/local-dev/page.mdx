Spin up the runner on a local cluster (`k3d`, `kind`, or OpenShift Local) to
evaluate TruStacks against real repositories without leaving your laptop.

## Cluster options

- `k3d` (lightweight, K3s in Docker)
- `kind` (Kubernetes in Docker)
- OpenShift Local (formerly CodeReady Containers)
