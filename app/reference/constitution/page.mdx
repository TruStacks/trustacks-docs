# Constitution

<Callout type="info">
  **Draft.** This page is a skeleton.
</Callout>

The constitution is the TruStacks-authored Rego foundation that every
proposal must respect. It is signed by TruStacks, distributed as an OCI
artifact, and verified by an init container before extract. Free at all
tiers. Non-waivable.

## What is in the constitution

- Path-touch rules · what files an agent is allowed to write
- Citation rules · every change carries a citation back to the rule that
  motivated it
- Required artifacts · SBOM, signature, scan report
- Required reviews · what categories of change need which approvals

## What is *not* in the constitution

Anything that varies between customers belongs in a Specialist Pack or your
overlay, not the constitution. Compliance-specific rules live in Specialist
Packs. Tribal knowledge lives in your overlay.
