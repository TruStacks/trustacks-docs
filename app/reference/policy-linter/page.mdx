# Policy linter

<Callout type="info">
  **Draft.** This page is a skeleton.
</Callout>

The policy linter is the compile-time tool that proves your overlay
ratchets stricter than the constitution and any Specialist Packs you have
layered on. Overlays that try to weaken a rule fail to build.

## Why it matters

The non-waivability of the constitution is cryptographically enforced, not
left to convention. Auditors can verify it. Your CISO can verify it. You
can verify it.
