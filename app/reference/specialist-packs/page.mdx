# Specialist Packs

A Specialist Pack adds domain expertise to the crew. Today every Specialist
Pack is a **specialist agent**: an agent the Coordinator consults during a gap
analysis, which returns findings grounded in your declared stack. There are
two.

| Specialist | What it covers | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Baseline Security** | Image scanning, SAST/SCA, secret scanning, SBOM and signing | Every tier |
| **SOC 2** | SOC 2 control families CC1 through CC9, with evidence hints | Included from Developer |

<Callout type="info">
  **No Specialist Pack Rego bundles ship yet.** Specialists advise; they do
  not add rules to the policy gate. The only rules your proposals are judged
  against are the [constitution](/reference/constitution) and any overlay
  rules you run yourself. HIPAA, PCI, FedRAMP, and ITIL are coming soon.
</Callout>

## When specialists run

Specialists run during a **gap analysis**, not during a code analysis or a
proposal. Start one from **Gap analyses**, or type `/gaps` in the Workspace
chat. The Coordinator decides which specialists are in scope and consults
them; the chat shows each consultation and reply as its own turn.

A finding can be remediated from the gap report, which creates a proposal
that goes through the same validation, policy gate, and human review as any
other. See [Proposal lifecycle](/reference/proposal-lifecycle).

## Baseline Security

Consults on the four security tooling categories of your
[environment profile](/reference/environment-profile): `image_scanning`,
`sast_sca`, `secret_scanning`, and `sbom_signing`. Returns findings with a
severity and tool candidates that fit what you already run. Included at every
tier.

## SOC 2

Returns findings mapped to a SOC 2 control family and control ID (for example
CC8.1), with the related tooling categories and hints about what evidence an
auditor would ask for.

The **Compliance** page shows, per SOC 2 control, how much evidence your
TruStacks activity has produced. It deliberately shows counts, not pass or
fail: whether a control is met is your auditor's call. **Audit reports**
(`#/audit`) exports that evidence as JSON or PDF on demand, with a SHA-256
digest in the header. Reports are not signed.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Tier gating while billing is off.** SOC 2 is included from the Developer
  tier. While billing is not configured on hosted TruStacks, the gate is not
  enforced and SOC 2 is available to every workspace.
</Callout>

## Coming soon

HIPAA, FedRAMP, PCI, and ITIL specialists. When regulatory packs ship they
may include Rego rules alongside the agent, curated by TruStacks so the
mapping to each control holds up in an audit. Nothing here is available
today, and the shape may change before it is.
