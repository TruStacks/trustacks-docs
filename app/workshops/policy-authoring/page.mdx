# Policy authoring workshop

<Callout type="info">
  **Draft.** This page is a skeleton.
</Callout>

Write your first customer-overlay rule. The Coordinator drafts the Rego,
generates test cases, and opens a PR against your overlay repository. You
review, refine, and approve. The agent does the syntax. You do the judgment.

## What you will build

A rule that codifies one piece of your team's tribal knowledge. The most
common starting point is a change-management rule (*"all production
deployments require an entry in our change calendar"*) or a deployment-window
rule (*"the legacy service deploys on Sunday nights only"*).

## Time required

Roughly 30 minutes.
