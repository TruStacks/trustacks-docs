# Compliance and audit reports

**Where:** **Compliance** in the sidebar (`#/compliance`); **Audit Reports**
at `#/audit`, reached from **Download an audit report (PDF) →** on
Compliance.

Compliance is about *proving what happened*. For how well your pipeline is
*set up*, see [Maturity](/guide/maturity). The two answer different
questions and are sold differently: Maturity is the core product, compliance
evidence is the first paid add-on.

## Compliance

{/* screenshot-placeholder: Compliance screen with the SOC2 evidence card listing controls and event counts for the last 90 days. Save as public/guide/compliance.png */}

### What you see

A **SOC2 evidence** card for the **last 90d**: one row per SOC2 control that
has evidence, with the control id, its title and a count of events
TruStacks recorded against it. Under it:

- **Download an audit report (PDF) →** opens Audit Reports.
- **Export full evidence pack →**, disabled for now.

If nothing was recorded in the window: *No evidence recorded in this window.
Evidence accrues as the crew runs analyses, proposals, and gap analyses.*

### What it means

Every governed action leaves a record: an analysis run, a proposal with its
policy verdict, a PR opened, a gap analysis. TruStacks maps those event kinds
to the SOC2 controls they evidence (for example, change-management controls
for governed proposals) and counts them.

<Callout type="info">
  **Evidence, not a verdict.** The card deliberately shows no pass, fail or
  "covered" marks. Evidence that a control was exercised is not the same as
  the control being satisfied. TruStacks reports what it observed; your
  auditor judges.
</Callout>

## Audit Reports

{/* screenshot-placeholder: Audit Reports form (control family SOC2, PDF, 7-day window) with the "Report download started" banner. Save as public/guide/audit-reports.png */}

### What you see

A form:

- **Control family**: SOC2 (the only family today).
- **Format**: **PDF (auditor-ready)** or **JSON (inline preview)**.
- **Window start** and **Window end** (defaults to the last 7 days).
- **Generate report**.

PDF downloads a file and confirms **Report download started**. JSON renders
a **Preview** of each control section on the page.

### What it means

The report is generated on demand from your workspace's recorded events
for the window. The PDF response carries a SHA-256 of its content in the
`X-TruStacks-Content-Sha256` response header (visible in your browser's
developer tools), which you can hand to your auditor alongside the file.

## What to do here

1. Before an audit, pick the observation window your auditor asked for
   and generate the PDF.
2. Use JSON to check what is in a window before you send anything.
3. Keep running your delivery through TruStacks: evidence accrues only
   from governed runs.

## How it connects

- Evidence comes from runs on [Applications](/guide/applications) (analyses
  and proposals) and [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses).
- The SOC2 specialist that comments during gap analyses is the same domain;
  see [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses).

## Beta limits

- **SOC2 only.** HIPAA, PCI, FedRAMP and other frameworks are coming
  soon.
- **The PDF is hashed, not signed.** It carries a SHA-256 header, not a
  cosign signature.
- **No stored history.** Reports are generated on demand; there is no list
  of past reports.
- **The full evidence-pack export is not built.**
- Once billing is live, SOC2 evidence is part of a paid subscription. While
  billing is not enabled, it is available to every workspace.
