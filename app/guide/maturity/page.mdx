# Maturity

**Where:** **Maturity** in the sidebar (`#/maturity`).

{/* screenshot-placeholder: Maturity hero with a silver badge, the verified/credited/potential bar, the tooling and behavior bars, two open-gap cards and the Path to gold card. Save as public/guide/maturity.png */}

Maturity answers *are we set up correctly, and what would make us better?*
It reads your most recent completed [gap analysis](/guide/gap-analyses) and
turns it into a score, a list of what is pulling the score down, and what
closing each gap takes. It is part of the core product, separate from
[Compliance](/guide/compliance).

## What you see

### Header

- An **Application** picker, shown when more than one Application has been
  scored. The default, **most recent run**, shows whichever ran last.
- A scope line: **Scored from *app*'s EnvironmentProfile.** If your other
  Applications declare their own profiles, it adds that they are not
  included in this score.
- **Run a new gap analysis →** (goes to [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses)).

If no gap analysis has completed, the screen says so and offers **Run a gap
analysis**.

### The hero

- **The tier badge**: bronze below 60%, silver from 60%, gold from 90%.
- **A three-part bar**:
  - **verified**: rows TruStacks has confirmed from evidence. The only part
    it stands behind.
  - **credited**: the extra credit from claims it could not check (claims
    count half).
  - **potential**: where the score would be if every claim were verified.
- **▲ / ▼ N pts since last run** (or *no change since last run*),
  comparing with the previous run of the same Application only. A drop is
  shown in amber, not red: a score becoming more honest is not a failure.
- **Tooling categories** and **Behaviors** bars, one segment per scored row.
- A list of any `practices:` keys in your profile that are not part of the
  vocabulary. They are not scored; check for a typo.

### Open gaps (left)

*What's pulling the score down.*

- **One card per unsatisfied tooling category**: the category, the rule
  that requires it, the recommended tool and why. **Draft pipeline PR**
  asks the crew to add that tool's step to your pipeline and opens a
  proposal (**Watch the crew work →**). The card says plainly that your
  score moves only once you also declare the tool in
  `environment-profile.yaml`, because that declaration is yours to make.
- **A behaviors card** (*N behaviors unearned*), split three ways:
  - **You declared these, and what we found disagrees** (disputed);
  - **You've declared these … and we couldn't verify them** (claimed,
    counted half; nothing is wrong with them);
  - **You have not declared these yet** (missing).

If nothing is pulling the score down, the card says everything scored is
covered.

### Path to gold (right)

What is left, in two voices:

- **Yours**: tooling gaps the crew can open PRs for now, and behaviors that
  need the practice in place and declared by you.
- **Ours**: declared rows that count half until TruStacks sees them in your
  pipeline, rows TruStacks has no way to observe yet (these can only ever
  count half, and it names them), and the score that closing every gap on
  the page would actually reach. If that ceiling is below 90%, it says gold
  is not reachable yet and why.

## What it means

The score is built from **rows**. There are two kinds:

- **Tooling categories**: *do you have the tool?* Declared under a category
  in your profile.
- **Behaviors**: *do you use it correctly?* Five keys under `practices:`:
  `tests_in_ci`, `linting_in_ci`, `manual_promotion_to_prod`,
  `non_root_containers` and `supply_chain_pinning`.

Only rows that some rule scores appear. Each row has a status:

| Status | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **verified** | Declared, and the evidence confirms it. | 1.0 |
| **claimed** | Declared, and there is no evidence either way. | 0.5 |
| **disputed** | Declared, and the evidence contradicts it. | 0 |
| **missing** | Not declared. | 0 |

The headline score is the weighted average; the tier comes from it. Evidence
comes from what TruStacks can observe: the workflow content in your
pipeline (a declared scanner is verified when its step is there) and the
constitution's verdicts on your latest judged proposal (for example whether
the workflow has a test step), plus the Code Reviewer's analysis. Missing
evidence never demotes a claim; only contrary evidence does.

The full model, and why it works this way, is in
[Verified, claimed and disputed](/concepts/verified-claimed-disputed) and the
[maturity scoring reference](/reference/maturity-scoring).

## What to do here

1. Start with **Open gaps**. For a tooling gap, **Draft pipeline PR**, review
   and merge the PR, then declare the tool in your profile.
2. For a missing behavior, put the practice in place, then add the line to
   `practices:`. For a disputed one, read the note: the evidence disagrees
   with your claim, so fix the pipeline or remove the claim.
3. Run a new gap analysis and compare.

<Callout type="info">
  **Why the score may fall.** When TruStacks gains a way to verify a row,
  or the rules gain a row, a score can drop without anything in your setup
  changing. That is the score becoming more accurate. The verified segment
  is the part you can show someone else with confidence.
</Callout>

## How it connects

- The input comes from [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses), the profile on
  [Stack](/guide/stack), and the rules on [Rules](/guide/rules).
- **Draft pipeline PR** creates a [proposal](/guide/applications#proposals).
- The headline also appears on [Overview](/guide/overview).

## Beta limits

- **Per Application, no roll-up.** There is no organisation-wide score.
- **Not every category is scored.** Several of the twelve tooling categories
  have no scoring rule yet; declaring them guides the agents but does not
  move the score.
- **Gold needs a current runner.** The ceiling depends on the rule inventory
  your runner reports. Reports from runs that predate the ceiling
  calculation show no ceiling and ask you to run a new analysis.
- Every tooling gap card is labelled **missing**, even when the underlying
  status is disputed.
- A "what we did not check" coverage view is planned, not built.
