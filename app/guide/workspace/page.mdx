# Workspace

**Where:** **Workspace** in the sidebar (`#/activity`). The older crew canvas
is one click away via **Crew view** in the header (`#/workspace`).

The Workspace is a chat with the **Coordinator**. Ask it about your setup in
plain language, or run a slash command. Every answer shows the records it came
from, and the Coordinator can't change anything: buttons in an answer only open
a page or start something you would start yourself.

## Ask about your setup

Type a question and press Enter. Good first questions:

- *Why am I bronze, and what would get me to silver?*
- *Why was my last proposal denied?*
- *Which of my rules are about production?*

The Coordinator answers from a snapshot of your own records, taken when you ask:

- your Application and its services;
- the latest gap report and every scored row, with its status and evidence note;
- your recent proposals and the constitution's verdict on each;
- the rules in force;
- your open findings.

### What you see in an answer

- **The answer**: short, with the numbers quoted exactly as your gap report
  and the constitution recorded them. The Coordinator never computes a score
  or a verdict itself; to change a score, run a new gap analysis.
- **Sources**: one chip per record the answer relies on, for example
  **Gap report: bronze, 23%** or **tests_in_ci: missing**. Each chip opens
  that record. The label is written by TruStacks from the record itself, not
  by the model.
- **Buttons**, when they help: **Run a gap analysis**, **Open the gap
  report**, **Open the proposal**, **Open the rule** or **Draft a rule**.
  Each button opens a page or starts the same thing you would start yourself.
  Approving and merging stay with you.
- **Not in your records**: when your records don't hold the answer, it says
  so instead of guessing. Those questions are kept so we can see what
  people need.

Follow-up questions continue the conversation (*"and what about gold?"*).

<Callout type="info">
  **Answers run on your runner, with your LLM key**, like every other agent
  run. Asking needs runner **0.2.15** or later. With an older runner, the
  chat says *No connected runner can answer questions yet. Upgrade your
  runner…* and links to [Runners](/guide/runners).
</Callout>

## The commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/gaps` | Runs a fresh gap analysis in the chat. The Constitution reports the gaps, the Coordinator consults **Baseline Security** and **SOC2**, and the run ends with a summary and **View the full report →**. `/audit` still works. |
| `/discover` | Takes you to your services to connect a repo. |
| `/rule new <plain-English description>` | Drafts an overlay rule from your description. The Coordinator proposes a rule id and writes the Rego. |
| `/rule new <rule_id>` | Starts a draft with an id you choose; your next message is what the rule should deny. |

## Drafting a rule

The draft arrives as a file block, `rules/<rule_id>/<rule_id>.rego`, with
**Copy** and **Download**, the Coordinator's reasoning, and a reminder of the
CLI steps to land it.

### Check what a draft would have blocked

Under every finished draft: **Would it have blocked my recent proposals?**

TruStacks replays your Application's 10 most recent proposals through the
policy engine twice: once with the rules you have today, and once with the
draft added. You see, for each proposal:

- **would be blocked**, with the draft's own message; or
- **passes this rule**;
- and, if the policy already refused it for another reason, *(already
  refused by …)*.

The policy engine decides every line; the assistant isn't involved. Use this
before you land a rule:

- **It blocks nothing you expected it to?** The draft may not be looking where
  your changes are. In the walkthrough for this guide, a draft that treated
  "production deploy" as one particular deploy action blocked 0 of 10
  proposals, because the service deploys a different way.
- **It blocks everything?** It may be too broad, or it may ask for something
  no proposal can provide.

The check refuses a draft that wouldn't actually be enforced. For example, a
draft whose package isn't `overlay.<name>` would load but never be consulted,
and it says so instead of reporting "blocks nothing".

Proposals from runner 0.2.15 on record exactly what the policy check was told
about their target, so a replay sees the same thing. Older proposals are
replayed with your Application's current target settings, and the result
notes that.

<Callout type="warning">
  **A draft is not live.** Nothing is saved to your repos and nothing is
  enforced from the chat. On the hosted service there is no supported way yet
  to deliver your own rules to your runner; loading them from your platform
  repo is planned. Today a draft lands through
  the `trustacks rule` CLI in a local environment; see the
  [policy authoring workshop](/workshops/policy-authoring).
</Callout>

Rule ids are `<namespace>.<name>`, lowercase with underscores, for example
`acme.requires_runbook_link`. TruStacks-reserved namespaces (`argocd`,
`constitution`, `posture`, `practice`, `proposal`, `trustacks`) are refused.
Keep the draft's `package overlay.<name>` line as written: the runner refuses
an overlay with a package outside `overlay.`.

## Your conversation is kept for 24 hours

Leave the Workspace and come back, and the conversation is still there,
including anything that finished while you were away. A question still being
answered picks up where it was.

What is kept is a list of references to your questions, gap analyses and rule
drafts, in your browser, per workspace and per user. The content is reloaded
from TruStacks when you return. **New conversation** clears it.

## Crew view

**Crew view** (header, top right) shows the newest analysis or proposal as a
flow of the agents that took part: who delegated to whom, each event in the
voice of the agent that produced it, and the most recent tool call with its
full parameters. A specialist that wasn't consulted isn't drawn.
**Back to the chat** returns here.

## Beta limits

- **Answers only know your records.** The snapshot holds scores, verdicts,
  rules and findings, not file contents. A question about what is in a file
  gets *Not in your records*.
- **The chat doesn't pick an Application.** With more than one Application,
  answers speak to what is common to all of them, and `/gaps` asks you to
  start the run from [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses), where you choose.
- **Rule drafting is single-pass.** You can't refine a draft turn by turn
  yet, and the Coordinator doesn't yet check that a rule is satisfiable or
  scope it to a tier. That is the next slice.
- The sidebar's **Crew online** strip is a static roster, not live status.
