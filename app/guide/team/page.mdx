# Team

**Where:** **Team** in the sidebar (`#/settings/team`).

{/* screenshot-placeholder: Team screen with the invite form open, two members and one pending invite. Save as public/guide/team.png */}

## What you see

- **Invite teammate**, which opens an email field and **Send invite**.
  The note under it: they are added to your organization and join the
  workspace on their first sign-in.
- **Members (N)**: everyone in the workspace, with **(you)** next to your own
  entry, each member's role, and **Remove** (with a confirm step) for
  others.
- **Pending invites (N)**: emails invited but not yet signed in, each with
  **Revoke**.

## What it means

A workspace is your organization's tenant in TruStacks. Inviting someone
adds them to your organization in the TruStacks sign-in service and emails
them. When they first sign in, they join the workspace and the invite is
marked accepted. Sign-in is by email and password.

Common messages:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *That person is already a member or already invited.* | Nothing to do. |
| *Enter a valid email address.* | Fix the address. |
| *Invites aren't set up for this workspace yet.* | Invites are not available on this deployment; contact TruStacks. |

## What to do here

- Invite the people who review and merge TruStacks PRs and who own your
  platform repo. In *Agents propose. Policy decides. Humans approve*, they
  are the humans who approve, by merging.
- Remove people when they leave. Removing a member does not touch their
  access to your Git provider or cluster, which you manage separately.

## How it connects

- Seats are part of your plan; see [Billing](/guide/billing).
- Everyone in the workspace sees the same Applications, runs and scores.

## Beta limits

- **No roles yet.** Every member is an owner with full access, including
  runner enrollment, LLM keys and billing. Application-level permissions
  are planned.
- **Seat limits are not enforced.** The Developer tier includes 5 seats;
  the app does not stop you inviting more during Beta.
- Google and Microsoft sign-in are not available on the hosted service.
