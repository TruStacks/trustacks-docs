# Billing

**Where:** **Billing** in the sidebar (`#/settings/billing`). A strip at the
top of every screen shows the program you're in (**Beta**) and your workspace
name. If you signed up with a Beta code and haven't added a card, it also
shows **Free until** a date, the days left, and **Add a card to keep going**.
A workspace on a time-boxed trial (or a workshop) sees **Trial** and the days
left instead; it turns urgent in the last five days.

## What you see

A status card:

- **Plan**: for example trial or paid.
- **Tier**: your subscription tier (for example Developer).
- **Subscription**: the Stripe subscription status, when there is one.
- **Trial ends**: the date, for a time-boxed trial or workshop workspace.
  Workspaces created through checkout are on a paid plan and don't show it.

Under it, one of:

- **Manage billing**: opens the Stripe customer portal (invoices, payment
  method, cancellation). Every workspace created through **Create a
  workspace** has a subscription, so this is what you see.
- **Upgrade**: opens Stripe Checkout to subscribe, for a workspace with no
  subscription yet (a trial or workshop).
- An amber note: **Billing isn't configured on this deployment**, and every
  feature is available with no subscription.

After Stripe sends you back, the screen confirms the outcome (*your
subscription is being activated*, or *Checkout canceled, no charge was
made*).

## What it means

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; TruStacks never sees your card.
A new workspace starts on the Developer plan through Stripe Checkout. With a
Beta code your first 45 days are free and no card is needed at checkout. Add
a card under **Manage billing** before the 45 days end and Stripe bills the
plan from then. Without a card, the subscription ends with the 45 days: you
can still sign in and see your workspace, but new work stops until you
subscribe. The Developer tier is the plan on
sale during Beta; see [pricing on trustacks.com](https://trustacks.com/pricing)
for what it includes. Higher tiers are not sold during Beta.

Your LLM usage is billed separately by your LLM provider under your own key
(see [Settings](/guide/settings)); it is not part of this bill. **During the
Beta it costs you nothing:** TruStacks provides the key. It stops working when
the Beta ends, and from then on usage is billed to your own key.

## What to do here

- On a Beta code: add a card under **Manage billing** before your 45 free
  days end (the date is in the strip at the top).
- On a trial: **Upgrade** before the trial ends.
- Use **Manage billing** to change your payment method or cancel.

## How it connects

- Seats on [Team](/guide/team) are tied to your plan once billing is
  enforced.
- So is the SOC2 Specialist Pack, which paid plans include: the SOC2
  specialist on [Gap analyses](/guide/gap-analyses) and the evidence on
  [Compliance](/guide/compliance). Pricing is at
  [trustacks.com/pricing](https://trustacks.com/pricing).

## Beta limits

- **Self-managed installs run unmetered.** The *Billing isn't configured*
  note appears only where billing is switched off (a self-managed install);
  there nothing is charged and nothing is gated. Billing is live on the
  hosted service.
- **Plan limits are not enforced in the app during Beta** (for example seat
  and service counts).
